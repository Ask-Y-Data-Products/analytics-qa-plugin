Defect: Moving the date picker changes every card except this one. Is it clear enough that 'Leads this month' always means the current calendar month and ignores the date picker?

Leads this month card ignores the date picker

What we saw: The definition uses ALL('dim_date') which strips any date filter. Moving the date slicer does not change this card. The 'Total Leads' and 'Leads (selected period)' cards next to it do change. A viewer who moves the date picker and sees those two cards change will assume this one changed too.

Why it matters: The card appears frozen during a demo if the date picker is moved. On a stale import it understates the current month without warning.
