Findings and business impact

FD-BARCHART-HIDES-GOOGLE-ADS: Leads by channel bar chart hides Google Ads

The bar chart has a visual-level filter excluding Google Ads, which accounts for 17% of total leads (2,006 of 11,724 in July). The table next to it shows all channels. A viewer comparing the two will find 2,006 leads missing with no indication.

Impact: Undercounts channel leads by 17% in the bar chart; misleading side-by-side comparison with the table.

C1-BARCHART-FILTER

evidence/runs/comp1_leads_v3/baseline/state.jsonevidence/comp1_leads.json
FD-MTD-IGNORES-SLICER: Leads this month card ignores the date picker

The Leads MTD measure uses ALL(dim_date) and TODAY(). It always shows the current calendar month regardless of the date slicer. On a stale import (data through Sep 3, today Sep 18), it shows only 3 days of a 18-day month. The cards next to it respond to the slicer, creating an inconsistency.

Impact: The card appears frozen during a demo if the date picker is moved. On a stale import it understates the current month without warning.

C2-IGNORES-SLICER C2-MTD-VALUE

evidence/runs/comp2_mtd/baseline/state.jsonevidence/comp2_mtd.json
FD-META-CRM-EXCEEDS-PLATFORM: CRM Meta leads exceed platform-reported leads on 20 days

On 20 days across the history, the CRM records more Meta leads than Meta's ad platform claims. The worst are Jul 7 (133 vs 60, ratio 2.22), Aug 11 (404 vs 105, ratio 3.85), and Apr 20 (88 vs 39, ratio 2.26). These are likely batch imports or attribution corrections, but without explanation they make the match rate chart misleading.

Impact: The match rate chart shows bars above 200%, which undermines trust in the comparison during a demo.

C3-RATIO-BOUND

evidence/comp3_meta.jsonevidence/detectors/comp3_probes/probes/meta-ratio-bound.json
FD-SPEND-BARCHART-FILTERED: Spend bar chart shows only 6 of 68 campaigns

The spend bar chart has a visual filter restricting it to 6 hard-coded campaign names. The table next to it shows all 68 campaigns. The bar chart total is a fraction of the page total.

Impact: Misleading side-by-side comparison; most spend is absent from the bar chart without explanation.

C4-BARCHART-FILTER

evidence/runs/comp4_spend_v2/baseline/state.jsonevidence/comp4_spend.json
FD-ORPHAN-CAMPAIGN-8591: Campaign 8591 has $18,400 in spend but no dimension name

Campaign 8591 exists in the fact table but maps to null in dim_campaign. In the bar chart (which groups by the dimension) this spend appears as a blank bar. In the table (which groups by the fact column) it shows the real name.

Impact: Spend is attributed to a blank campaign in the bar chart; viewers cannot identify which campaign it belongs to.

Probe dax.assert failed: Observed 1126 == expected 68: does not hold.

Impact: Stated invariant does not hold in the imported population; the affected visuals inherit the error.

Coverage and limitations

  • A Power BI Desktop message banner blocked slicer interaction; all four cycles used baseline-only captures at the default July 2026 range. Date-change and channel-selection experiments were designed but could not execute. The requested baseline of Jul 1-13 was tested via DAX engine queries only, not through the rendered UI.
  • The spend bar chart and table show different campaign name sources (dim_campaign vs fact_ad_spend_allocated_daily). The RELATED probe for orphan spend failed because fact_ad_spend_allocated_daily does not have a direct relationship to dim_campaign that supports row-context RELATED. The orphan was confirmed by comparing SUMMARIZECOLUMNS output from both sources.
  • No RLS or OLS inspection was performed; the model was accessed from a local Desktop session with unrestricted access.
  • The report has multiple additional pages and many measures not reviewed in this demo-scoped investigation.
