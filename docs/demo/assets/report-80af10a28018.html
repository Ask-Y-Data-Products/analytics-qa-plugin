FD-TIMEZONE-UTC: Inquiry date keys are UTC-based, not Toronto time

Impact: Late-night inquiries in Eastern time are attributed to the wrong calendar day. Daily counts near midnight differ from what a Toronto-based analyst expects. This affects all daily charts and the is_current_mtd page filter.
